The world

With this on, the loaded classes run oldest season first and each one hands its conference map, program strength, coaches and star returners to the next, a later class's upperclassmen play the earlier seasons on their real rosters — and every tab, and the export, show that world rather than a fresh re-simulation. College season

A class flavor says what kind of PLAYERS this year has. A narrative says what kind of SEASON they played — a wide-open year, a mid-major surge, a scoring explosion, a chaotic sideline. Two or three of them stack.

Pace and scoring environment move possessions; shooting efficiency moves what a possession is worth. Pace, both environment dials and stat randomness apply to Division I only — every professional league abroad has its own pace and game length.
